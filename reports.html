A year on St. Andrews Bay

A rough guide to what we're doing through the year, drawn from how we actually fish and hunt.

SeasonOn the water
SpringRedfish and speckled trout on the flats with top‑water and soft plastics. When tides are favorable, live bait trips for 6‑plus pound trout and reds.
SummerLive bait trips continue. Tarpon cruising the beaches for sight fishing. Offshore reef trips for red snapper, king mackerel, amberjack and cobia.
AutumnBig schools of feeding redfish and swarms of hungry trout in the shallows. Pompano and Spanish mackerel on light tackle.
WinterDiver duck season, November through January: redheads, bluebills, canvasbacks and buffleheads. Cast & Blast days pair a morning hunt with an afternoon on the flats.
