Policies

If the captain cancels

The client is not responsible for any fees.

Cancelling within 24 hours

The client is responsible for the full fee of the charter, as we cannot rebook the trip.

Cancelling within one week

The client will be charged a $100 rebooking fee.

Children under six

Must wear a PFD while we are underway. Please let us know ahead of time so we can make PFD arrangements.

Groups & corporate

We can arrange group or corporate charters. We have a great network of other area guides that we coordinate with to accommodate large groups.

Where we meet

There are several local boat ramps to begin your trip. We'll arrange the most convenient location, depending on where you are staying and where we'll be fishing.

Duck hunting rates

Hunt rates are quoted by phone or email. See the duck hunting page for what is included, or write to capttodd@backbayadventures.com.

Reservations

Reserve your day on the bay
